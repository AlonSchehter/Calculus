# Calculus activity header convention

Use **Geometric Concepts** as the reference for the top area of every calculus
activity. Keep the compact header at the upper right: the dark rounded integral
home logo, author name and email, language button, and shared presentation-pen
control. Do not introduce a full-width navigation bar or put the activity title
in that bar.

Place the activity title below this group, at the top of the right sidebar.
Use the same bold Arial treatment, centered alignment, and consistent spacing
before the activity/function tabs. Desktop reference: a 40px-high logo group
at 12px from the top, and a 21px title below it. Keep the title and sidebar in
the same location in Hebrew and English; English tab labels are left-aligned
and LTR. Keep primary controls within the viewport and retain responsive sizing.

Surface transparency controls use the label **Transparency / שקיפות**.
100% means fully transparent, 0% means opaque. Surfaces and Function Types
(including Function Composition's embedded renderer) default to 65%.
Provide a compact slider and a click-to-edit percentage with Enter to apply
and Escape to cancel. Preserve the established palette and shading.

All editable formulas accept plain or backslash sqrt with braces or parentheses, and frac with braced pairs, paired parentheses or comma-separated arguments. Shared FormulaInput normalizes nested expressions before editor submission, including embedded activities. Parentheses size to their enclosed contents. Category order: Series, Geometry, Derivatives.
